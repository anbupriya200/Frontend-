HCL Technologies

About

HCL (Hindustan Computers Limited) is a global IT services and consulting company founded in 1976. Headquartered in Noida, India, its subsidiary, HCLTech, is India’s third-largest IT firm. Employing over 223,000 people across 60 countries, HCL provides AI, digital, cloud, engineering, and software services.

Services

HCLTech provides global IT and business services, including digital transformation, cloud optimization (CloudSMART), AI/GenAI integration, enterprise software, and engineering services. In India, they drive local delivery and talent development, while HCLFoundation supports elite para-athletes with equipment and scholarships under their 'Sports for Change' initiative
