CSE

CSE stands for Computer Science and Engineering, a popular 4-year undergraduate engineering degree. It covers programming, algorithms, and software-hardware systems.


IT

Information Technology (IT) is the use of computers, networks, and storage to create, process, store, and exchange electronic data. It core components include hardware, software, and data management.


ECE

Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) is a core engineering branch focusing on electronic circuits, communication systems, and signal transmission. It bridges hardware and software to power modern devices


EEE

EEE stands for Electrical and Electronics Engineering. It is a core engineering discipline that blends the study of large-scale power generation, transmission, and electrical machinery with modern electronic circuits, microcontrollers, and automation systems


Mechanical

A mechanical description explains how a physical device or machine is built, how its parts fit together, and how it moves or works. It focuses on physical parts, materials, shapes, and the forces that make the system function.
