About trip

The best time to visit Thailand is between November and February, when the weather is comfortably cool and dry across most regions. For tourist entry, Thailand offers a seamless 30-day visa-free entry for citizens of many countries, including India.

Key Trip Highlights & Details

Must-See Sights: Explore Bangkok's Grand Palace and Wat Arun, relax at Railay Beach in Krabi, or shop at the massive Chatuchak Weekend Market.

Weather: The cool season ranges from 25–32°C, while the hot season from March–May peaks near 40°C (ideal for the April Songkran Festival).
