Until Love Sets Us Apart

Until Love Sets Us Apart is a heart-wrenching romance novel by Aditya Nighhot. It follows Rohit (an aimless student) and Aisha (a focused perfectionist) who fall in love during their MBBS studies. Their plans for marriage are shattered when an unforeseen, violent tragedy tears them apart, changing everything.

The narrative begins with a framing story: a writer trying to investigate a murder case for his next novel, only to discover it involves Rohit taking the law into his own hands after Aisha is brutally attacked.

The Mystery Framework

The novel opens with an MBBS student-turned-author who, while searching for inspiration for a new book, stumbles upon a string of murders. Intrigued by the case and the man behind it, he tracks down the suspect’s friend, Alok, to uncover the motive. Through Alok's narration, the reader is taken into a massive flashback that explains the origin of the tragedy.

The Love Story

The flashback chronicles the college romance between Rohit and Aisha. The two are complete opposites: Rohit is an imperfect, laid-back, and somewhat aimless boy, while Aisha is a highly focused, mature perfectionist. Despite these differences, they fall deeply in love during their medical journey and even plan to get married after completing college.
