About

Mailam (Constituency No. 71) is a Tamil Nadu state assembly constituency in Viluppuram district. It comprises portions of the Gingee and Tindivanam taluks and is part of the Arani Lok Sabha constituency. The incumbent MLA is C. Ve. Shanmugam of the AIADMK party.

The constituency is predominantly rural and heavily reliant on agriculture, particularly the production of paddy and sugarcane. For detailed historical statistics and performance trends, you can explore the IndiaVotes Mailam Page or check the full candidate profiles on the MyNeta Comparison Chart.

History

Formed in 2008 following constituency delimitations, the Mailam Assembly constituency is a rural, general-category seat in Tamil Nadu's Viluppuram district, comprising parts of the Gingee and Tindivanam taluks. It has a history of intensely contested elections between the AIADMK, DMK, and PMK parties

In the most recent elections, AIADMK's C. Ve. Shanmugam won the seat with a margin of 30,041 votes, securing about 43.6% of the vote share. He defeated TVK candidate A. Vijay Niranjan. Prior to this, the constituency saw seesaw political battles: PMK's C. Sivakumar won in 2021 by a narrow margin of 2,230 votes, and DMK's R. Masilamani secured the seat in 2016 by defeating the AIADMK.

Location

The Mailam Assembly constituency is located in the Viluppuram district of Tamil Nadu. Bearing constituency number 71, it comprises portions of the Gingee and Tindivanam taluks and is an assembly segment under the Arani Lok Sabha constituency.
