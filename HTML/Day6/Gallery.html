Nature

Nature, in its broadest sense, refers to the physical world, the universe, and all living organisms within it. It encompasses the laws, phenomena, and ecosystems that shape life on Earth, from microscopic plankton blooms to vast forests.

Bike

The term "bike" refers to either a bicycle or a motorcycle. Bicycles rely on pedaling power for eco-friendly, low-maintenance travel. Motorcycles use engines with varying displacement (cc), torque, and horsepower for faster, motorized transport.
