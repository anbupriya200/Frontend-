Company Name : Alibaba Group Holding Limited

About Company

Founded: June 28, 1999

Founder: Jack Ma

Mission: Make it easy to do business anywhere.

Services

Alibaba.com: A global platform for wholesale trade. It lets businesses buy and sell bulk items internationally.

Alibaba Cloud: A major cloud computing service. It provides data storage and web hosting for businesses.

Location

Global Headquarters: Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China.

Global Presence: Alibaba operates in over 200 countries and regions. The company maintains major regional offices in Hong Kong, Singapore, and the United States.
